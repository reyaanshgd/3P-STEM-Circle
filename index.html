The 3P Learning Model

Our educational framework anchors science and technology in meaningful local contexts.

1. Problem-Based

Students engage with critical, real-world challenges—developing analytical skills and critical thinking by designing authentic solutions.

2. Project-Based

Learners gain hands-on technical skills through long-term creation, collaboration, and building tangible prototypes.

3. Place-Based

Education is tied to local environments, culture, and community assets, fostering civic engagement and relevant impact.
